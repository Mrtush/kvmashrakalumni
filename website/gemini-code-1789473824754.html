About Our Community & Association

Kendriya Vidyalaya Mashrak was established to impart quality education and holistic discipline in Saran, Bihar. This community portal serves as a bridge connecting past alumni batches, respected teachers, and present junior students to foster mentorship, reunions, career guidance, and school nostalgia.
